Frequently asked

Is it really free?

Yes. The free tier gives you one personalized 4-week plan per month with no signup required. Pro ($9/mo) unlocks unlimited generations and extras.

Do I need to create an account?

No. You can generate, save and share your plan without signing up. An account only matters if you want unlimited Pro generations.

How long does a plan take to generate?

About 5 seconds. We send your goals to Claude AI and stream the result back as a clean, printable plan.

What equipment do I need?

Whatever you have. Tell the planner if you train at a full gym, with dumbbells at home, or bodyweight only — the plan adapts to what's available.

Is the plan safe if I have an injury?

You can declare injuries in the questionnaire and the AI will avoid contraindicated movements. This isn't medical advice — check with a physio for serious conditions.

Can I cancel Pro anytime?

Yes. One click in your account, no calls, no retention pages. Refunds within 14 days, no questions asked.
